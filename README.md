## 1. Dubai Tenancy Security Deposits (RERA & RDC)
* **Governing Legislation:** Dubai Law No. 26 of 2007 (as amended by Law No. 33 of 2008) regulating the relationship between landlords and tenants in the Emirate of Dubai (ordinary wear and tear vs. chargeable damage).
* **RDC First Instance Monetary Claim Fee:** **3.5%** of the claimed amount (minimum **AED 500**, maximum **AED 15,000**), excluding certified Arabic legal translation and service-center typing/admin charges.

## 2. Dubai Car Rental Security Deposits (DET Circular 1/2024)
* **Governing Rule:** Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (**DET**) **Circular No. 1/2024** mandates the release of customer security deposits within **30 days** of vehicle return.
* **Escalation Channels:** Consumer Rights Dubai (`consumerrights.ae`) and issuing-bank card chargebacks (for captured charges without itemized Salik, RTA traffic fine, or signed damage inspection proof).

## 3. UAE Airline Delay & Baggage Claims
* **Governing Frameworks:** UAE GCAA Passenger Welfare regulations (duty of care) and the **Montreal Convention** (baggage delay/loss liability cap updated to **1,519 SDR**).
* **Strict Notice Windows:** **7 days** from receipt for damaged baggage; **21 days** from delivery for delayed baggage.
* **Free Template:** [UAE Flight Delay & Lost Baggage Claim Email Template](https://evidencepack.visisellai.com/templates/uae-flight-delay-lost-baggage-claim-email)

## 4. Commercial B2B Invoices (DIFC Small Claims Tribunal)
* **Jurisdiction & Threshold:** Where DIFC jurisdiction applies (by contract clause or written opt-in), monetary claims up to **AED 500,000** can be filed at the **DIFC Small Claims Tribunal** (**5%** filing fee, minimum **USD 100**) without external legal representation.
